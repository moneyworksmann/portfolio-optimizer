Words used on this page
bp (basis point)
One hundredth of a percentage point. 100 bp = 1%. “Rates +250 bp” means the 10-year yield goes from, say, 4.2% to 6.7%.
Yield curve gap
The 10-year Treasury yield minus the 3-month bill yield. When it’s negative (“inverted”), recessions have often followed.
VIX
The market’s “fear gauge”, based on options prices. Around 15 is calm; above 30 is stress; it hit 80 in March 2020.
Monte Carlo
Running thousands of random but realistic futures and counting how often each outcome happens, instead of making one prediction.
Fat tails
Allowing rare, big crashes to happen more often than a simple bell curve would. Closer to real markets.
1-in-20 bad case
Only 5% of simulated futures end lower than this.
